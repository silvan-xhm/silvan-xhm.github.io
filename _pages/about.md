<section class="intro" id="about-me" aria-label="About Me">
  <p>I am a master's student in the School of Mechanical Engineering at <a href="https://english.bit.edu.cn/">Beijing Institute of Technology</a> and a research intern at <a href="https://www.horizon.auto/">Horizon Robotics Lab</a>. Previously, I completed my undergraduate studies at the College of Engineering, <a href="https://www.cau.edu.cn/">China Agricultural University</a>.</p>
  <p>My research interests focus on Humanoid Motion Control, Vision-Language Navigation (VLN), World Models, and Robotic Table Tennis. I welcome opportunities for collaboration in these areas.</p>
</section>
